# Product Context & Business Logic

## Core Problem
- Address "Fixed Pay for Unequal Work" and the "Hidden Work Gap" within educational and corporate environments.
- Maintain work-accountability ledger using token-backed credits.

## Trusted Work-Organization Model (Core Philosophy)
- WorkLedger is a **trust-based personal work organizer and reminder platform**.
- It is **NOT** faculty attendance surveillance, biometrics, GPS, or daily proof requirements for structured teaching sessions.
- Faculty receive recurring weekly timetable reminders and self-declare completion in 2 steps.
- Monthly progress is composed of structured timetable sessions (e.g. 75% target weight) and unstructured institutional initiatives (25% target weight).
- If scheduled target is 75 credits and weight is 75%, total target is 100 credits (`75 / 0.75`).
- 85% monthly salary authorization threshold activates on or after Day 26 of the cycle for HOD approval and on-chain settlement.
