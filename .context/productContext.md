# Product Context & Business Logic

## Core Problem
- Address "Fixed Pay for Unequal Work" and the "Hidden Work Gap" within educational and corporate environments.
- Maintain work-accountability ledger using token-backed credits.

## Non-Monetary Token Liquidity Engine
- Members earn token-backed credits by completing tasks (structured and unstructured).
- Target threshold: monthly target credits (typically 85% of monthly target).
- Repayment framework: fallback loans from the organization's `LOAN_POOL` to cover shortfalls.
